🐛 Report a Bug

Found something that doesn't work right? Please email us with the following details so we can fix it quickly:

  • A brief description of what happened
  • What you expected to happen
  • Your device model and iOS version
  • Steps to reproduce the issue (if possible)
  • Screenshots or screen recordings (if helpful)

Response Times

We aim to respond to all emails within 24–48 hours on business days. For urgent issues, please include "URGENT" in your email subject line.

Before You Contact Us

A few quick things you can try:

  • Restart the app — force-close Rootling and reopen it
  • Update to the latest version — check the App Store for updates
  • Restart your device — this resolves many common issues
  • Check iCloud status — visit Apple System Status if sync isn't working
  • Check our FAQ — your question may already be answered
